Document: Laboratory X Safety Procedures
Note: This is a fictional document created to test search functionality.

Wear a lab coat and safety goggles when entering the laboratory.
Experiments involving chemicals must always be performed by at least two people.

The laboratory is open from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
After use, turn off equipment and fill in the usage log.

If you notice an equipment malfunction, stop work and contact Sato, who is responsible for safety management.
Safety training is held on the first Monday of every month.
